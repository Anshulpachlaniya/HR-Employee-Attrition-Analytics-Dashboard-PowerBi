# HR Employee Attrition & Workforce Analytics Dashboard

![HR Analytical Dashboard](hr_dashboard_preview.png)

## Project Overview
This Power BI project analyzes employee data for **1,403 staff members** to understand employee turnover (**227 exits, 16.2% attrition rate**). The dashboard helps HR teams track key workforce metrics across departments, job roles, salary slabs, age groups, and experience levels to improve employee retention.

---

## Business Problem
The HR team wanted clear answers to the following questions:
1. What is our current employee attrition rate and active headcount?
2. Which departments and job roles are losing the most employees?
3. How do salary slabs, age, experience, and job satisfaction affect turnover?

---

## Key Metrics
* **Total Employees:** 1,403
* **Active Employees:** 1,176
* **Attrition Count:** 227
* **Attrition Rate:** 16.2%
* **Average Employee Age:** 36.97 years
* **Average Experience:** 7.05 years

---

## Key Findings
* **High-Turnover Departments:** **Sales** has the highest attrition rate (**25%**), followed by **Human Resources (23%)** and **Administration (21%)**. **Operations** has the largest workforce (**510 employees**) with the lowest turnover (**8%**).
* **Most Affected Job Roles:** **Laboratory Technicians (59 exits)**, **Sales Executives (55 exits)**, **Research Scientists (44 exits)**, and **Sales Representatives (32 exits)** make up over 83% of all departures.
* **Salary Trends:** The **6–10 LPA** group has the most total exits (**71 employees**), while the **10+ LPA** group has the highest exit rate (**19.3%**), showing that high pay alone does not prevent turnover.
* **Experience & Age:** Employees aged **26–35** form the largest group (**580 staff, 41.3%**). Attrition spikes early at **1 year** of experience and again around **6–10 years**.
* **Gender Breakdown:** The company consists of **841 Male (59.9%)** and **562 Female (40.1%)** employees.

---

## Recommendations
1. **Support Sales & Lab Teams:** Review targets, workload, and incentives for Sales roles and Laboratory Technicians to reduce the highest volume of exits.
2. **Improve Early & Mid-Career Retention:** Add 6-month and 1-year check-ins for new hires (to fix the Year-1 spike) and clear growth paths for staff with 6–10 years of experience.
3. **Conduct Stay & Exit Interviews for Senior Staff:** Engage employees in the **10+ LPA** bracket and **Job Satisfaction Level 3** group to address non-pay factors like career stagnation or burnout.
4. **Replicate Operations Management Practices:** Share leadership and scheduling practices from **Operations (8% attrition)** with high-turnover departments.
